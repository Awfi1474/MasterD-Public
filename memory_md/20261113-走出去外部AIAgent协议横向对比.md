## 关键结论(照实·带原文)
① A2A与MCP互补非竞争(官方Appendix B):
 "MCP focuses on model-to-tool interaction, while A2A focuses on
  agent-to-agent interaction. They are complementary"
② 两家都捐给Linux Foundation(官方新闻)
③ 身份机制: 只有ANP用DID|MCP/A2A故意把认证留在协议外(交给传输层)
   = 印证老六今早查的"A2A官方不绑身份方案"
④ MCP三原语: tools(可执行)/resources(可读)/prompts(可复用模板)
⑤ MCP传输: stdio(本地)/Streamable HTTP(2025-06-18起取代SSE)
⑥ MCP协商: initialize交换protocolVersion+返回capabilities
