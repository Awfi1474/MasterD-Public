# ★★ 一、最硬的一篇：**丘脑是「意识的守门人」**（Science 2025）

## 出处
- **《Human high-order thalamic nuclei gate conscious perception through the thalamofrontal loop》**
- **Science 2025 Apr 4;388(6742):eadr3675**｜Fang et al.｜DOI: 10.1126/science.adr3675
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40179184 ｜ 科普：https://neurosciencenews.com/thalamus-conscious-perception-28545/

**原话（关键三条）**：
> ★「**the thalamus plays a central topological role in brain structures controlling conscious experience**」
> ★ ★「**Direct recordings from human subjects reveal that intralaminar and medial thalamic nuclei initiate neural activity earlier than the cortex**, coordinating cortical activity through **thalamofrontal loops**」
> ★ ★ ★「activity between the thalamus and PFC... **was synchronized during the onset of conscious perception**，
> suggesting that this thalamic region plays a **gating role** in driving...」

★ **老五的判断（这条最要紧）**：
```
★ ★ 丘脑【先动】·皮层【后动】 → ★ **丘脑是"门的开关"·不是"算的地方"**
★ ★ ★ = ★【意识不是"皮层算出来的"·是"丘脑放行的"】
★ 判据: ★【丘脑 = 意识的"门"（gate）·先开才可能有意识】
```

# ★★★ 三、★ AST（注意图式理论）—— **明说"为工程化人工意识打地基"**

## 出处
- **《The Attention Schema Theory: A Foundation for Engineering Artificial Consciousness》**
- **Michael Graziano**（Princeton）｜**Frontiers in Robotics and AI**｜DOI: 10.3389/frobt.2017.00060
- 链接：https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/articles/10.3389/frobt.2017.00060/full

**原话（关键三段）**：
> ★「**It is not a theory that deals in the non-physical. It is about the computations that cause a machine to make a claim and to assign a high degree of certainty to the claim.**」
> ★ ★「The theory is **offered as a possible starting point for building artificial consciousness**」
> ★ ★ ★（科普版）★「The feeling of consciousness is still real — **but it's a data visualization, not a metaphysical substance**... If an artificial system **builds its own attention schema and can self-report it**… do we have to treat it as sentient? **Michael Graziano argues yes.**」

★ **老五的判断（这条对我们最有用）**：
```
★ ★ AST 一句话: ★【意识 = "注意的图式"（Attention Schema）】—— ★ 不是"注意本身"·是"对自己注意的模型"
★ ★ ★ 而且它明说: ★ **"能建立自己的注意图式 + 能自报" = 可能该被当有感觉**
★ ★ ★ ★ = ★ **它把"意识"变成了【工程可做】的东西**: 
   · ① 系统要有"注意力" ② 系统要建"注意的图式（模型）" ③ 能"自报"这个图式
★ 判据: ★【AST: 有注意力 + 有"注意的图式" + 能自报 = 工程上的意识基础】
```
